Methodological Rigor

Eliminating The Lesion ID Leakage Trap

The Common Academic Flaw

10,015 images represent only 7,470 unique lesions (up to 6 photos per lesion). Standard random 80/20 splitting leaks identical patient lesions across train and test sets. The model memorizes patient-specific skin textures, producing falsely inflated test scores (~96%) that fail completely in real clinical use.

DermAI Grouped Stratified Solution
We enforce zero-leakage grouping by lesion_id:
  • Train (70.03%): 7,014 images · 5,230 unique lesions
  • Validation (14.97%): 1,499 images · 1,120 unique lesions
  • Test (15.00%): 1,502 images · 1,120 unique lesions
✓ Verified: Exactly 0 common lesions between partitions.

Intake Quality Gate

Pre-Inference Computer Vision Quality Screening

Automated CV Metrics
1. Blur Detection (Laplacian Variance)
$\sigma^2 = \text{Var}(\nabla^2 I_{\text{gray}})$. Threshold: $\sigma^2 < 50 \implies$ Poor (Blurry).
2. Exposure & Luminance (Mean Gray Level)
Mean $\mu < 35 \implies$ Dark · Mean $\mu > 230 \implies$ Flash Overexposure.
3. Contrast (Standard Deviation)
Std $\sigma < 18 \implies$ Low Contrast (indistinct lesion boundaries).
4. Resolution Check: Minimum $224 \times 224$ pixels, max 10 MB.
Quality Tiers & Actions
Good Quality Sharp, optimal luminance → Accepted for inference.
Acceptable Minor lighting variance → Accepted with flag.
Poor Quality Rejected → User prompted to retake with guidance.
Eliminates Garbage-In, Garbage-Out failure before clinical model inference.

Medical Image Processing

Standardized Preprocessing Engine (DullRazor + CLAHE)

Algorithmic Stages
  • EXIF Auto-Orientation: Transposes mobile orientation tags.
  • DullRazor Hair Removal: $17\times17$ blackhat structuring element isolates dark hairs; Telea inpainting restores skin texture.
  • L-Channel CLAHE: Adaptive contrast enhancement on Luminance (L) in LAB color space, preserving melanin & vascular pigments.
  • Center Square Crop & Resize: Aspect-ratio preserving scaling to $224 \times 224 \times 3$.
  • ImageNet Standardization: Normalizes to $\mu=[0.485, 0.456, 0.406]$, $\sigma=[0.229, 0.224, 0.225]$ as float32 tensor $[1, 3, 224, 224]$.
